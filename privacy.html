Privacy Policy

Stride · last updated 30 September 2026

Stride is a social step-competition app. This policy explains exactly what it collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

Health and motion data

With your permission Stride reads your daily step count from Apple Health. Nothing else from Health is read, ever. With your permission it also counts today's steps with your iPhone's motion sensor (Motion & Fitness), so the number moves while you walk; Stride keeps the higher of the two counts for today. Step totals are used to score your leagues, races and streaks, so your daily step total is uploaded to our server and shown to people you compete with — your league group, your friends, and anyone in a race with you. Individual samples, workouts, locations and routes are never read or uploaded. Health and motion data is never used for advertising and is never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Account data

  • Email address — to sign you in. Sign in with Apple may give us a private relay address instead; if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us and nothing else.
  • Username, display name and profile photo — shown to other players. You choose all three.
  • Friends, friend requests, blocks and reports — to run the social features and keep them safe. A report (its reason and any note you add) is emailed to our moderation inbox.
  • Races you create — their name and rules, shown to the people in them.
  • Your Bib's look — the colors and kit you pick with Pro, shown to other players.
  • League and race results — your history, and your place in other people's.
  • Purchases — whether you have Stride Pro, and until when. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card. RevenueCat checks your App Store purchases for us using your Stride user ID, and our server keeps each Apple transaction's ID, product and expiry date so Pro can't be faked.

Diagnostics

Stride uses Apple's MetricKit to record crashes on your device. That data stays on your device unless you choose to include it in a support email. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker in the app.

Where it lives

Account and competition data is stored on Supabase, our hosting provider, and is protected by row-level security so you can only read what you're entitled to see. Purchases are checked by RevenueCat, report emails are sent through Resend, and notifications are delivered by Apple. Widgets and Live Activities show data kept on your iPhone.

Your control

  • Delete everything: Settings › Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, steps, results, friendships and photo. It cannot be undone. Purchase records kept by Apple and RevenueCat stay, so you can restore Pro.
  • Revoke Health access at any time in the Health app › Sharing › Apps. Stride keeps working; leagues simply stop scoring.
  • Turn off live steps in Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness. Today's steps then come from Health only.
  • Blocking: a blocked account can't see you, contact you, or find you in search.
  • Export or correction: email us and we'll send or fix your data.

Children

Stride is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you before the change takes effect.
